## 3. QUESTIONS TO ASK MY BROKER

1. "Based on my income of $115k, $10k card limit, and no HECS, what is your estimate of my borrowing capacity?"
2. "As a PAYG employee, which lenders are most likely to offer the best rate at 80% LVR for an investment property?"
3. "Is there anything in my profile that could complicate or delay my approval?"
4. "Are there any lender restrictions on this suburb or property type at 80% LVR?"
5. "Is LMI required at 80% LVR? What if I went to 82% LVR — is there a significant cost difference?"
6. "What rate can I expect for a 5-year IO investment loan at 80% LVR today?"
7. "If I take IO for 5 years, what does the repayment look like when it reverts to P&I?"
8. "What offset structure gives me the most flexibility on this investment loan?"
9. "What is your expected timeline from application to formal approval for a straightforward PAYG investor?"
10. "What could delay my approval? What should I prepare in advance?"

## 4. FLAGS TO RAISE

- Suburb has riverside location — flag with broker: any lender restrictions due to flood zone?
- Property is a 3-bed house — standard security, should have broad lender appetite
- IO preference — confirm which lenders offer competitive IO rates for investment at 80% LVR
