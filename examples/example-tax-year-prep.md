## 3. CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS vs MAINTENANCE

> These are preliminary classifications only. Your registered tax agent makes the final determination.

| Item | Cost | Preliminary classification | Reason | Flag for accountant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| New hot water system | $2,400 | Likely maintenance | Like-for-like replacement of failed unit — restoring to original condition | Ask to confirm |
| Repaint exterior | $5,800 | Likely maintenance | Repainting existing painted surface, same colour/material — restoration | Ask to confirm |
| New kitchen | $34,000 | Likely capital improvement | Full kitchen renovation — improved beyond original condition and adds value | Yes — confirm treatment |

**For the new kitchen:** This is almost certainly a capital improvement, not immediately deductible. Your accountant will determine whether it is added to the cost base (if property is sold eventually) or depreciated as a building improvement under Division 43. Bring receipts and a description of what was replaced.
