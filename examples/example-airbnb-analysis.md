---

## 5. LENDER APPETITE FLAGS

- Most Australian lenders will NOT accept STR income for serviceability on a $850,000 purchase
- Borrowing capacity will be assessed on long-term rental income equivalent ($680/week) if at all
- Some specialist lenders may accept STR income with 2 years of Airbnb income history — verify with broker
- LVR restriction: some lenders cap at 70% LVR for holiday or STR properties — verify

## 7. QUESTIONS FOR PROFESSIONALS

**For your mortgage broker:**
- "Which lenders will consider STR income for serviceability on a QLD holiday apartment? What LVR applies?"
- "Is this property classed as a holiday property by any lenders? Does that affect the rate?"

**For the local council:**
- "Are there any STR restrictions or permit requirements for short-term rental accommodation at this address?"
- "Is this property in a tourism zone? Does that affect permitted uses?"

**For the body corporate / OC manager:**
- "Do the current by-laws permit short-term rental letting (Airbnb-style) at this complex?"
- "Has any motion been passed or proposed to restrict short-term letting?"

**For your accountant / tax agent:**
- "Does $73,470 gross STR revenue trigger GST registration obligations?"
- "What proportion of furnishing and operating expenses is deductible if I use the property personally for some weeks?"
