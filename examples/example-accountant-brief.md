## 3. COMMON MISTAKES TO DISCUSS

- Stamp duty: not deductible in year of purchase — adds to cost base
- Sinking fund contributions: not deductible in QLD

- Borrowing costs: treatment varies — verify with tax agent

3. "Should we commission a QS depreciation schedule? What annual claim should we expect for a 2005 townhouse?"
4. "What plant and equipment can we depreciate from settlement — carpet, blinds, hot water system?"

8. "Is there any benefit to changing the ownership split or structure for a future purchase?"
