# Skill — Loan Application Fact-Find Prep

**Stage:** 2 — Finance
**Hook:** Walk into the broker meeting with the whole picture already organised — your position *and* what you actually want from the loan. A complete fact-find gets you a better conversation and a faster application.
**Use when:** You're preparing to apply for (or refinance) a home or investment loan and want to assemble the full fact-find a broker works through — your financial position plus your objectives and requirements.

---

## Purpose

This skill helps a borrower assemble a complete, broker-ready **fact-find**: personal and household details, employment, income, assets, liabilities, living expenses, and — the part borrowers most often skip — their **objectives and requirements** (what they want the loan to do). A licensed broker must document objectives and requirements to meet their Best Interests Duty, so bringing them ready makes the process smoother.

It organises information for a professional conversation. It is **not** credit advice, an application, or a serviceability assessment.

## How it's assessed

A fact-find is about completeness and honesty, not calculation. Two cross-checks matter:

- **HEM cross-check on living expenses:** lenders compare your declared expenses to the Household Expenditure Measure (a benchmark by household size and location) and use the **higher** of the two. Declaring unrealistically low expenses won't help — it just gets overridden. Be realistic.
- **The 2-year employment rule:** if you've been in your current role under ~2 years, lenders usually want the previous role too — have it ready.
- **Objectives & requirements** are not optional niceties: they shape which loan is appropriate, and a broker must record them. Anticipated changes (e.g. going to one income, retirement before the loan term ends) affect suitability — surface them now, not at settlement.

> This skill does not compute borrowing capacity. For that, use [Borrowing Power](borrowing-power.md); for an independent servicing check, use [Servicing Compliance Check](servicing-compliance-check.md).

## Output contract

Return exactly these 6 sections:

### 1. APPLICANT & HOUSEHOLD SUMMARY
Applicant(s), DOB, marital status, dependants and ages, residency. Flag anything missing.

### 2. EMPLOYMENT & INCOME
Per applicant: employer/ABN, employment type, time in role (and previous role if under ~2 years), and each income type listed separately (base vs variable). Label variable income (overtime/bonus/commission/rental) — lenders treat it differently.

### 3. ASSETS & LIABILITIES (position snapshot)
Two clean lists: assets (savings, shares, super, property values, vehicles) and liabilities (mortgages with fixed/variable + repayment, personal/car loans, HECS/HELP, card **limits**). Net position noted as an estimate.

### 4. LIVING EXPENSES (with HEM note)
Expenses by category (or total), with a note that the lender will use the higher of declared vs HEM — so figures should be realistic.

### 5. OBJECTIVES & REQUIREMENTS
The borrower's priorities, loan features wanted, timeline, and **anticipated changes** with rough timing and dollar impact. This is the Best-Interests-Duty core — make it explicit.

### 6. GAPS & QUESTIONS FOR YOUR BROKER
What's still missing or to verify, plus tailored questions (e.g. "given I plan to drop to one income in ~2 years, which structure suits?", "is interest-only appropriate for my goals?").

## Safety boundaries

- This is information-gathering and preparation only — **not credit advice**, not an application, and not a serviceability or borrowing-capacity assessment.
- Never state a borrower will be approved, or suggest a specific lender or product is "best" — a licensed broker assesses suitability under their Best Interests Duty.
- Encourage realistic living-expense figures — understating them is counter-productive (HEM overrides) and undermines the fact-find.
- Handle this information securely and only with the applicant's consent — it is sensitive personal and financial data.

---

## Professional review prompts

- Ask your **licensed broker (ACL holder)**: "Based on this fact-find and my objectives, which loan structures suit — and why?"
- Ask your **broker**: "Given my anticipated changes, is there anything about my timeline or structure I should reconsider?"
