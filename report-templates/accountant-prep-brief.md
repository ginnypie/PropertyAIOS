## 3. COMMON MISTAKES TO DISCUSS

- Stamp duty — not deductible; it is part of the cost base
- Capital improvements vs repairs — different tax treatment
- Sinking fund / capital works contributions (strata) — not deductible
- Borrowing costs — treatment varies; verify with your tax agent

## 4. QUESTIONS FOR YOUR ACCOUNTANT

**About negative gearing:**
1. "Given my income and tax rate, what is the estimated after-tax weekly holding cost?"
2. "Are there any expenses I'm missing from my deductible list?"

**About depreciation:**
3. "Should I commission a QS depreciation schedule for this property?"
4. "What plant and equipment can I depreciate from settlement?"

**About capital gains:**
5. "If I sell in [X] years, what is the estimated CGT position?"
6. "How would the proposed CGT discount changes (if passed) affect my position?"

**About ownership structure:**
7. "Should this property be in my name, joint, trust, or SMSF? What are the land tax implications?"

**About land tax:**
8. "What is my land tax position across all my properties in [state]?"
