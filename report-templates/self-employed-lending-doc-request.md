# Self-Employed Lending — Accountant Document Request (Email Template)

**PropertyAIOS — General Information and Educational Preparation Only**
**A reusable template. Fill the [PLACEHOLDERS]. Not credit advice. Confirm the exact document list with the licensed broker or lender.**

---

## How to use

Use this when a self-employed or business-owner borrower needs their accountant to supply the financials and ATO documents for a lending application. **Only send it with the client's authority**, and request that documents come back through a **secure** channel — never plain email attachments. Tailor the document list to the client's entity structure (see the [self-employed-lending-prep skill](../skills/self-employed-lending-prep.md)).

---

## Email template

> **Subject:** Document request — [CLIENT NAME] loan application

Dear [ACCOUNTANT NAME / FIRM],

We're currently supporting [CLIENT NAME(S)] with their lending needs at [BROKERAGE NAME], and they have authorised us to contact you to request the documents required for their application.

We'd greatly appreciate the following at your earliest convenience.

**Documents required — for [YEAR 1] and [YEAR 2]:**

- Financials for all businesses and entities
- Tax returns for all entities
- Individual tax returns (all applicants)
- Latest year Notice of Assessment (NOA)

**Individual ATO portals:**

- Income Tax Account statement — *only if there is a debit balance on the latest Notice of Assessment*

**Business ATO portals (all entities):**

- 12-month running balance on the Income Tax Account (ITA) and Integrated Client Account (ICA)
- A front-page summary of all tax accounts

**Business Activity Statements (BAS):**

- All ATO-lodged BAS since the end of the last financial year

**Ownership confirmation:**

- Confirmation of shareholders / beneficial owners in the business

Please upload the documents for [CLIENT NAME] securely via [SECURE UPLOAD LINK]. Kindly do not send financial documents as plain email attachments.

Thank you for your help.

Kind regards,
[BROKER NAME]
[BROKERAGE NAME] · [CONTACT DETAILS]

## Disclaimer

> This is a general document-preparation template only, not credit advice and not a lender's definitive document list — actual requirements vary by lender, applicant, and entity structure, and must be confirmed with the holder of an Australian Credit Licence (ACL) or the lender. Client financial documents must only be requested with the client's authority and shared through a secure channel.
