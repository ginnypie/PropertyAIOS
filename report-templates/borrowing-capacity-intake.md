# Borrowing Capacity — Client Information Request (Email Template)

**PropertyAIOS — General Information and Educational Preparation Only**
**A reusable template. Fill the [PLACEHOLDERS]. Not credit advice. Actual borrowing capacity is assessed only by a licensed broker or lender.**

---

## How to use

Use this to collect, in one go, the information needed to prepare an **indicative** borrowing-capacity estimate (feeds the [borrowing-power skill](../skills/borrowing-power.md)). Ask the client to return the details through a **secure** channel — this is sensitive financial information. The estimate that follows is deliberately **conservative** and is not a credit assessment or pre-approval.

---

## Email template

> **Subject:** Information needed to estimate your borrowing capacity

Hi [CLIENT NAME],

To estimate your borrowing capacity for a home loan, could you please provide the following:

- Name
- Marital status
- Number and age of dependants
- Employment — PAYG or self-employed, and how long in current employment
- For PAYG — gross annual income
- For self-employed — taxable income on the previous 2 years' Notices of Assessment
- Estimated value of existing property/s
- Balance of current mortgage/s, fixed or variable, and monthly repayment
- Balance of any car loans / personal loans, and monthly repayment
- Credit card provider, limit/s and balance/s
- Amount of Family Tax Benefit received — Part A & B
- Any savings held in a bank account and/or shares
- Balance of super

Please return these securely via [SECURE LINK]. If anything is an estimate, that's fine — just note it.

Thanks,
[BROKER NAME]
[BROKERAGE NAME] · [CONTACT DETAILS]

## Disclaimer

> This is a general information-gathering template only, not credit advice. Any borrowing-capacity figure produced from it is a conservative, indicative estimate — not a credit assessment, serviceability approval, or pre-approval. Only the holder of an Australian Credit Licence (ACL) — a licensed mortgage broker or lender — can assess actual borrowing capacity. Collect and store this information securely and only with the client's consent.
