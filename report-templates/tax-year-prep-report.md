## 3. CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS vs MAINTENANCE

> Your registered tax agent makes the final determination. The table below is a preliminary flag only.

| Item | Cost | Preliminary classification | Flag for accountant |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ITEM 1] | $[X] | Likely maintenance / Likely capital / Unclear | Yes / No |
| [ITEM 2] | $[X] | Likely maintenance / Likely capital / Unclear | Yes / No |

**Rule of thumb for accountant discussion:**
- Restoring to original condition = likely maintenance (deductible now)
- Improving beyond original condition = likely capital (depreciate or add to cost base)
- Initial repairs to defects at purchase = capital (add to cost base, not deductible)
- Any single item over ~$300 = discuss with your accountant before classifying
