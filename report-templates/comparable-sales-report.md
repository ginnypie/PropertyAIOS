## 5. IS THE ASKING PRICE REALISTIC?

**Verdict: [OVER / FAIR / UNDER]** — by [$X] / [X%] against the implied range.

| Condition | Result |
|---|---|
| Price above the high | **OVER** — priced above the evidence |
| Price within low–high | **FAIR** — supported by the evidence |
| Price below the low | **UNDER** — below the evidence |

- **If OVER:** how far above the high, and the negotiation insight (e.g. "the gap to the mid is your opening argument — bring the three closest comps").
- **If FAIR:** where in the range it sits (bottom / middle / top) and what that means for likely competition.
- **If UNDER:** how far below the low, and the checklist of reasons to rule out **before** treating it as a bargain — defect, easement, flood/fire overlay, short lease, urgent/forced sale.
