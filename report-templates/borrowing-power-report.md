## 4. WHAT INCREASES / REDUCES YOUR CAPACITY

> Each lever below is indicative only — the actual effect depends on the specific lender.

**Increases capacity:**
- Closing or reducing a credit card **limit** (even an unused limit is a commitment)
- Paying down personal / car loans
- Choosing a lender with more generous policy for your income type
- Adding assessable rental income (shaded, verify with broker)
- A lower actual interest rate

**Reduces capacity:**
- Higher credit card limits (even if unused)
- Additional dependants
- HECS/HELP repayments
- Higher declared living expenses
- Rate rises lifting the assessment rate
