# The Professional Review Model

PropertyAIOS is built on a clear four-layer model:

```
AI prepares.
Humans review.
Licensed professionals advise.
The investor decides after proper advice.
```

This is not a limitation of the system — it is the design. Each layer does what it is best at.

---

## The four layers

### Layer 1 — AI prepares

The AI skill system organises research, tests assumptions, identifies risks, surfaces questions, and produces structured outputs the user can bring to their professional conversations.

**What AI does well here:**
- Works through checklists without forgetting steps
- Identifies common risk patterns
- Structures information in consistent formats
- Generates the full list of questions a user might not think to ask
- Runs scenarios and stress-tests quickly

**What AI does not do:**
- Give advice
- Access live market data (without integrations)
- Know the user's specific circumstances with certainty
- Replace professional judgment

### Layer 2 — Humans review

The user reviews the AI output. They decide:
- What is relevant to their situation
- What assumptions are correct or need adjusting
- What additional information they have that the AI didn't
- What questions they want to prioritise in professional conversations

This review step is essential. AI outputs are starting points, not final answers.

### Layer 3 — Licensed professionals advise

Professionals review the user's prepared questions and information and give licensed, specific advice:

| Professional | Role | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Mortgage broker | Borrowing capacity, loan structure, lender selection | ACL (Australian Credit Licence) |
| Accountant / tax agent | Tax implications, depreciation, negative gearing, CGT | Registered tax agent |
| Solicitor / conveyancer | Contract review, title search, legal risks | Legal practitioner |
| Buyer's agent | Property selection, negotiation strategy | Real estate agent licence |
| Property manager | Rental appraisal, vacancy risk, tenancy management | Real estate agent licence |
| Quantity surveyor | Depreciation schedule | Member of AIQS |
| Property valuer | Formal valuation | Certified Practising Valuer (API) |
| Financial planner | Portfolio strategy, SMSF | AFSL (Australian Financial Services Licence) |

### Layer 4 — The investor decides

After professional advice, the investor makes their own informed decision. The AI system, the preparation work, and the professional advice all feed into this decision — but the decision belongs to the investor.

---

## Why this model matters

### For compliance

Keeping AI in the preparation layer means the outputs are general information, not advice. Advice is what licensed professionals provide in Layer 3.

### For better outcomes

Users who prepare before professional consultations get more value from those consultations. They ask sharper questions. They catch more issues. They spend less time on basics and more time on their specific situation.

### For professional partnerships

Brokers, accountants, solicitors, and buyer's agents who recommend PropertyAIOS to their clients benefit from better-prepared clients. Every prepared client is a more efficient client.

## How to communicate this model to users

Use plain language:

> "PropertyAIOS helps you prepare. Your mortgage broker, accountant, and solicitor give you the actual advice. PropertyAIOS is the preparation that makes those conversations count."

Or:

> "Think of it as showing up to the appointment with your homework done."

Or:

> "AI can run the analysis. It can't approve the loan, lodge the return, or review the contract. That's what the professionals are for."

---

## Adapting this model for other niches

The four-layer model works in any property market. The specific professional roles differ by jurisdiction:

| AU | UK | US |
|---|---|---|
| Mortgage broker (ACL) | Mortgage adviser (FCA) | Mortgage broker (NMLS) |
| Solicitor / conveyancer | Solicitor | Real estate attorney |
| Tax agent | Tax adviser | CPA / tax attorney |
| Buyer's agent | Buying agent | Buyer's agent / realtor |
| Property valuer | RICS surveyor | Licensed appraiser |

Replace the roles and licences for your market, but keep the model: **AI prepares, professionals advise, investor decides.**
