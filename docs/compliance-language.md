# Compliance Language Standard

How to write copy, skill output, and report content in PropertyAIOS so it stays on the right side of the line: **general information only, never personal advice.** Apply this to every skill, agent, command, report, and prompt card, alongside the disclaimer blocks in [`disclaimers/`](../disclaimers/).

---

## Foundation rule

All content is **general information only**. It is not personal financial, credit, tax, or legal advice. Every borrower's and investor's situation is different, and licensed professionals do the advising.

---

## What content CAN say

- How lenders, the ATO, or the market generally assess a scenario
- Why outcomes differ between people
- What factors may be considered
- General education about lending, tax, and property frameworks
- General explanation of documentation and process
- General differences in lender or policy treatment

## What content CANNOT say

- "You will be approved" / "guaranteed approval" / "approved today"
- "Best rate" / "lowest rate"
- Personal financial, credit, or tax advice
- A specific lender, product, suburb, or property recommendation as "the right choice"
- Specific outcome promises
- "No documents needed" / "everyone qualifies" / "no risk"
- "Act now" / "don't miss out" (pressure language)

---

## Safer language

**Use:**
- "lenders may consider…"
- "subject to assessment"
- "depends on lender / ATO / state policy"
- "general information only"
- "this may be relevant"
- "some borrowers / investors in this position…"
- "speak with a qualified broker / accountant / solicitor"
- "estimate — verify with…"

**Avoid:**
- guaranteed · approved today · best rate · lowest rate
- no documents needed · everyone qualifies · no risk
- act now · don't miss out

---

## Numbers

Never present a figure as accurate. Label every number an **estimate**, **assumption**, or **illustration**, and name who verifies it (broker, accountant, valuer, council, lender). Always show a **range** rather than a single figure for values, capacity, or returns. This mirrors the repo rule in [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md): *never generate numbers as if they are accurate*.

---

## Required disclaimer line

Every output carries a disclaimer from [`disclaimers/`](../disclaimers/). The minimum general line:

> This content is general information only and does not constitute personal financial, credit, tax, or legal advice. Every situation is different — speak to the relevant qualified professional before making a decision.

---

## Why this matters

Property investment and lending workflows touch financial, credit, tax, and legal domains — all regulated. Staying in "general information" language is what lets an open, AI-assisted tool be genuinely useful **without** crossing into advice that requires a licence. When in doubt, soften the claim and defer to the professional.
