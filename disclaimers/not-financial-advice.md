# Disclaimer — Not Financial Advice

## When to use

Include this disclaimer on any output that:

- Discusses property as an investment
- References cash flow, yield, returns, or growth
- Compares investment structures or strategies
- Discusses portfolio composition or balance
- References superannuation (SMSF)
- Compares buy vs rent, invest vs owner-occupy
