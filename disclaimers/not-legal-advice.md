# Disclaimer — Not Legal Advice

## When to use

Include this disclaimer on any output that:

- Reviews or summarises a sale contract
- Discusses vendor statements, section 32, or disclosure statements
- Discusses easements, covenants, caveats, or encumbrances
- References strata bylaws, body corporate rules, or leasehold terms
- Discusses tenancy law, lease agreements, or landlord obligations
- References building permits, planning approvals, or zoning
- Discusses estate planning, ownership structures, or trusts

> This output does not constitute legal advice. It is general information only and does not take into account your specific legal position or the terms of any particular contract, title, or agreement.
