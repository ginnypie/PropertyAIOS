# Disclaimer — Not Tax Advice

## When to use

Include this disclaimer on any output that:

- Discusses negative gearing or tax deductions
- References depreciation (building allowance, plant and equipment)
- Discusses capital gains tax (CGT) or the CGT discount
- References land tax
- Discusses income tax treatment of rental income
- References the proposed 1 July 2027 CGT changes
- Discusses SMSF tax treatment
- References duty or transfer tax

---

## Standard block

> This output does not constitute tax advice. It is general information only and does not take into account your personal tax situation, income, deductions, or obligations.
>
> Tax treatment of property investments depends on individual circumstances and is subject to change. References to negative gearing, depreciation, capital gains tax, land tax, and other tax matters are general in nature.
>
> Before making any decision that has tax implications, seek advice from a registered tax agent. In Australia, tax advice must be given by a registered tax agent under the Tax Agent Services Act 2009. PropertyAIOS is not a registered tax agent and does not provide tax advice.

## For proposed CGT changes specifically

> **Note on proposed CGT changes:** Any references in this system to proposed changes to the capital gains tax discount or cost-base indexation rules describe measures that may have been proposed at the time content was written. The status of proposed legislation changes over time. Always confirm the current status of any proposed tax measure with a registered tax agent before relying on it. Do not make decisions based on proposed legislation without professional confirmation that it has passed and applies to your situation.

---

## For depreciation specifically

> Depreciation schedules and capital works deductions must be prepared by a qualified quantity surveyor and reviewed by a registered tax agent. Depreciation estimates in this output are illustrative only and will vary based on construction date, fit-out, and ATO guidance at the time of lodgement.
