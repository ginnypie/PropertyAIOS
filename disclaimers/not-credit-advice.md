# Disclaimer — Not Credit Advice

## When to use

Include this disclaimer on any output that:

- Discusses borrowing capacity, serviceability, or loan size
- Compares LVR scenarios (80% vs 90%)
- Discusses loan structure, interest-only vs P&I
- References LMI (lenders mortgage insurance)
- Discusses refinancing, equity release, or debt consolidation
- Prepares questions for a mortgage broker
- References lender policy, credit assessment, or pre-approval

---

## Standard block

> This output does not constitute credit assistance within the meaning of the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (Cth). It is general information only and does not take into account your personal credit situation, income, liabilities, or lending requirements.
>
> Borrowing capacity, loan structure, and lender suitability depend on individual circumstances and specific lender credit policy. Figures presented are illustrative assumptions only.
>
> Before making any borrowing or financing decision, seek credit assistance from the holder of an Australian Credit Licence (ACL). PropertyAIOS does not hold an ACL and does not provide credit assistance.

## For borrowing capacity outputs specifically

> **Important:** The borrowing estimates above are illustrative starting points only. They are not a credit assessment, pre-approval, or indication of what any specific lender will approve. Different lenders apply different income shading, expense benchmarks, and credit policy. Only a licensed mortgage broker can match you to the right lender and give you an accurate indication.
