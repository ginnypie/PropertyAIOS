Appraisal Approach & Reasoning

This appraisal uses the direct comparison approach against recent waterfront sales in the Bluewater Marina canal estate. Figures are indicative and rely on the comparable evidence shown overleaf being correct.

The anchor is 12 Apollo Quay — same street, same 600 m² land — sold $1.60m (Aug 2025). The estate brackets the subject: a floor at 54 Harbour Drive ($1.30m, identical 5/3/2 but weaker) and 46 Marina ($1.50m); a ceiling at 9 Marina ($1.80m) and the larger 121 Harbour ($1.92m).

The fifth bedroom and pool lift the subject above the $1.5m comps; an unconfirmed interior and unproven marina berth hold it below the $1.8m+ reads. Land-rate check: $1.62m ÷ 600 m² ≈ $2,700/m², matching both 12 Apollo Quay and the AVM. Same-street comp, estate cluster and AVM all converge near $1.6m.

Confidence: Medium–High. To sharpen: confirm interior condition, confirm marina berth/pontoon, and pull the Cairns council flood / storm-tide overlay (portal auto-flag is unreliable for canal blocks).
