## Page headline

The questions you need answered before you sign anything.

## Subheading

Due diligence isn't one checklist. It's four professional conversations, and most buyers only have one.

---

## What you get back

1. **Contract and Title Flags** — questions for your solicitor before you sign
2. **Building and Pest Flags** — what to ask your inspector before they go in
3. **Strata and Body Corporate Flags** — the numbers most buyers miss (sinking fund, special levies, disputes)
4. **Suburb and Zoning Flags** — flood maps, planning overlays, development applications
5. **Finance Flags** — lender restrictions on the property type or postcode
6. **Red Flags Summary** — everything in one table with severity ratings
7. **Pre-Exchange Checklist** — don't exchange without ticking these

---

## How to use it

Run the due diligence scan after you've inspected and are seriously considering making an offer. Take the output to your solicitor as the starting point for their review — not as a replacement for it.
