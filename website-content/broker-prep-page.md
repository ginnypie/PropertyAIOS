## What you get back

1. **Document Checklist** — what to bring to the meeting
2. **Your Financial Summary** — a one-page briefing the broker can read
3. **10 Specific Questions** — about your borrowing profile, this property, loan structure, and process
4. **Flags from Your Research** — property-specific issues to raise proactively
5. **What to Expect** — how to tell if the broker conversation is a good one

---

## The point

A broker who knows your situation in the first 10 minutes can spend the next 50 minutes on strategy, lender selection, and structure. A broker who has to extract your basic financial position spends 40 minutes on information-gathering.

This pack lets you walk in already calibrated.
