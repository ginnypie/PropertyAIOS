## How to use the prompts

1. Choose the skill that matches your current stage
2. Copy the prompt
3. Paste it into Claude (claude.ai)
4. Replace every field in [square brackets] with your actual details
5. Read the output — the questions you can't answer are the gaps to research
6. Take the output to the relevant professional
