---

## What you get back

Run the Suburb Research skill and you get:

1. **Suburb Snapshot** — property types, land sizes, distance from employment, what's actually here
2. **Employment and Economic Base** — who employs people in this suburb and how diversified that is
3. **Infrastructure and Liveability** — transport, schools, healthcare, retail, and planned developments
4. **Supply and Demand Signals** — development pipeline, days on market, vacancy signals
5. **Lender Appetite Flags** — whether this postcode or property type attracts lender restrictions
6. **Risk Overlays to Verify** — flood zone, bushfire, industrial proximity, contamination — what you need to check
7. **Suburb Score Summary** — a simple table you can read in 60 seconds

---

## Who this is for

Investors who want a structured read before committing to inspections. Buyers who want to compare two suburbs side by side. Anyone who's been burned by a "great suburb" that turned out to have a complication they didn't know to look for.

---

## What you do with it

You take the output to three conversations:

- Your **buyer's agent** — for current on-the-ground intelligence and comparable sales
- Your **mortgage broker** — to confirm whether this postcode has any lender restrictions
- Your **property manager** — to verify the rental demand and vacancy signals

The research is yours. The professional judgment is theirs.
