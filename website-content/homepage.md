## The three steps

**Step 1 — Analyse the property**
Run a structured first pass on any listing before you book an inspection. Check the suburb signals, the cash flow assumptions, and the red flags worth investigating.

**Step 2 — Finance-check your position**
Understand what a lender actually sees before you walk into a broker meeting. Test your borrowing position, model the loan structure options, and prepare the questions that get the conversation off generic and onto your specific situation.

**Step 3 — Prepare for the professional conversations**
Every skill ends with the questions to take to your broker, accountant, solicitor, or buyer's agent. Arrive prepared. The professionals provide the advice — PropertyAIOS helps you ask the right questions.

---

## What PropertyAIOS is not

PropertyAIOS does not provide financial advice, credit advice, tax advice, legal advice, or property recommendations. It is a preparation and research tool. The professionals you speak with give the advice.
