## The questions most investors don't ask

- "What is the after-tax weekly cost of holding this property for my income level?"
- "Should I commission a depreciation schedule? What annual claim should I expect?"
- "How does the proposed CGT change affect my planning?"
- "What is my land tax position across my portfolio in this state?"

These are the questions that change the numbers. This prep pack helps you ask all of them.
